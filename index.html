About the Proposal

Poorna Market is an urban-level design proposal. The aerial view and street scenes illustrate a connected market experience in a broadly Indian context.

The five map points indicate approximate design zones, not surveyed coordinates. Images are design visualizations rather than photographs of a completed place; measured dimensions and performance claims await verified drawings.

The 360° tour presents ten interpretive standing points, two in each zone. Select an arrow to jump between them in a browser or compatible WebXR headset. They show design intent, not a surveyed route or photographic site documentation.

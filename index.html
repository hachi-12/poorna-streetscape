About the Proposal

Poorna Market is presented here as an urban-level design proposal. The aerial view and street scenes illustrate a connected market experience in a broadly Indian context.

The five hotspots are approximate design zones on a concept render. These visuals are not a survey map or photographs of a completed place. No unverified measurements, capacities or environmental performance claims are presented.

The explorable 3D market is a conceptual model. Its building forms are diagrammatic, with proposal images displayed as spatial exhibits rather than 360° panoramas. A compatible WebXR headset enables controller travel and selection.

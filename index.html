Poorna Market · Visakhapatnam

A breathable bazaar.

A proposal for the city’s oldest market: open edges, shaded lanes, a planted roof, and a street of hawkers that runs straight into the market.

Explore in 3D ↓ Take the 360° tour ↗
01

The idea

Poorna Market works because it is open. You can read the lanes at a glance, find shade where you want to linger, and goods spill right up to the street. The proposal keeps that spirit and lets the building breathe with it.

  1. 1

    Open edges

    Stalls, steps and thresholds meet the street, so the market reads as part of the city, not a wall against it.

  2. 2

    Shaded, airy routes

    Covered lanes and a central plaza give people places to pause and room for air and light to move through.

  3. 3

    Planted layers

    Green terraces and the planted roof soften the building and lift a garden above the market.

02

The side entrance

A brick gateway with a woven shade canopy and a marigold toran opens the market onto the hawker street. The forecourt in front is a place to arrive, meet and spill out, not just a doorway.

  • Brick piers echo the market’s existing brickwork.
  • Woven canopy shades the threshold and lets air through.
  • Planted forecourt with trees on either side of the gate.
03

The hawker street

The side road becomes a pedestrian bazaar. Produce stalls line the market edge, push-carts with umbrellas sit in the middle, and the shophouses opposite keep their open fronts, signboards and balconies, all strung together with festival bunting.

Shops, stalls and people are an illustrative context model added to the proposal, not a survey of the existing street.

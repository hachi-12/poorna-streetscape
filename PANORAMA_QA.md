# Poorna Market 360° tour assets

The ten files in assets/panoramas/ are generated **design visualizations**, not photographs or surveyed documentation. The arrows illustrate conceptual connections between standing points, not measured walking distances.

Each image was generated with the built-in image generation tool using the existing roof render as a 2:1 composition reference and the matching zone render as an architectural reference. The shared prompt requested a full-sphere equirectangular view at pedestrian eye height, continuous geometry through 360°, a centered horizon, joined left/right edges, and the proposal's brick screens, shade, planting, stalls, limestone, laterite, deep green, and turmeric palette. Distinct location prompts specified the city threshold, gateway interior, produce arcade and stalls, central market court and shaded gathering space, textile threshold and lane, and two roof terrace viewpoints.

All files are 1774 × 887 pixels. Four 90° rectilinear diagnostic views at yaw 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270° were inspected for each source. They show market or surrounding-street content in every direction with no blank sector. The top/bottom poles and seams remain interpretive AI renderings; inspect them in the actual browser and headset before release.

The source images and diagnostic contact sheets are retained outside the published site in E:\atchu\qa-panorama. Run python E:\atchu\qa-panorama\verify_tour.py to recheck dimensions, paths, five pairs, reciprocal links, connectivity, and required HTML controls. Browser, mobile, controller, and live GitHub Pages verification remain required before publishing.
